Fried potatoes are a simple yet popular dish made from sliced potatoes cooked until golden and crisp. Their appeal comes from the contrast between the crunchy exterior and the soft, fluffy interior. The natural flavor of the potatoes is enhanced by frying, which gives them a rich and savory taste.

Salt is usually added to highlight their flavor, making them a perfect side dish or snack. Fried potatoes are versatile and enjoyed on their own or paired with sauces and main dishes. Their simplicity and satisfying texture make them a classic comfort food loved worldwide.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Vegetable oil (for frying)
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Prepare the potatoes: Peel the potatoes if desired and cut them into evenly sized sticks.
  2. Heat the oil: Pour vegetable oil into a deep pan or fryer and heat it until hot.
  3. Fry the potatoes: Cook the potatoes in hot oil until they turn golden and crispy.
  4. Drain: Remove the fries and place them on paper towels to absorb excess oil.
  5. Season and serve: Sprinkle with salt and serve hot.
